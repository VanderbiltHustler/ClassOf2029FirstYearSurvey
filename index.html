The First Year Survey

What is "The First-year Survey"?

Every year, The Vanderbilt Hustler conducts an in-depth survey to understand who Vanderbilt’s newest class is. What defines them? What led them to Vanderbilt? Are they planning on joining Greek life? The Hustler tried to answer all these questions and more about this new class.

  • 208 participants
  • 45 questions
  • 12.8% response rate

What are 1-2 words that you hope will describe your Vanderbilt experience?
